About Me...

Hi, I'm Eshiet Ekemini, a community manager and growth strategist with 4 years of experience building passionate, loyal communities for SaaS brands, consumer apps, gaming studios, and Web3 projects.

I believe community is the most powerful growth channel a brand can have. My approach centers on authentic engagement, structured programming, and data-backed decision-making, transforming passive audiences into vocal brand advocates.

From community platform selection and launch, to ambassador programs and cross-platform content calendars, I own the full community lifecycle and work as a true partner to the brands I serve.

Beyond community management, I also have experience building and maintaining websites and web applications. I help brands keep their websites updated, functional, and aligned with their goals while supporting their overall digital presence.
